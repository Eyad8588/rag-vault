# Nimbus Help Center: Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I share a notebook?
Open the notebook, click Share and enter your teammate's email. Shared notebooks sync in real time on the web, iOS and Android apps.

## Does Nimbus work offline?
Yes. Since Nimbus 2.0 every note is stored on your device and synced when you are back online.

## Which plans are available?
Nimbus has Free, Pro and Team plans. The Team plan adds admin controls and single sign-on.

## How do I contact support?
Use the in-app chat or write to the Orion Labs support team through the Help Center. We answer within one business day.
