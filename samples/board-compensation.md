# Board Minutes: Compensation and Acquisition (Strictly Confidential)

Do not distribute. Present: Sara Ahmed (CEO), David Cohen (CFO), Priya Nair (board chair).

## Acquisition
The board discussed the offer from Helix Systems to acquire Orion Labs at a valuation of 180 million dollars. David Cohen recommended rejecting the offer and continuing talks with Quartz Ventures about a Series B round.

## Compensation
The board approved a salary of 310,000 dollars for the new CTO, Li Wei, starting in February, plus 1.5 percent equity. Bonuses for the platform team were capped at 12 percent this year.

## Layoffs
A reduction of 14 roles in the support team in Toronto was approved for Q2, pending legal review.
