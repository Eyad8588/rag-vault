# Nimbus 2.0 Launch Announcement

Orion Labs today announced Nimbus 2.0, the new version of its collaborative note-taking app. Nimbus 2.0 adds offline sync, end-to-end encrypted sharing and a redesigned mobile app for iOS and Android.

"Small teams told us they wanted something fast that works on a plane," said Sara Ahmed, CEO of Orion Labs. The release was built by the Orion Labs team in Cairo and Berlin.

Nimbus 2.0 is available today on the Free, Pro and Team plans. Customers on the Team plan get admin controls and single sign-on. The public beta of the Nimbus API, which lets developers build integrations, opens next month.

Orion Labs will demo Nimbus 2.0 at the Web Summit in Lisbon and at its community meetup in Cairo.
