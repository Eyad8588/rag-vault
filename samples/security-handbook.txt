Orion Labs Security Handbook (internal)

All employees must enable hardware security keys for their Orion Labs accounts. Lost keys must be reported to the security team within 24 hours.

Production access is granted through Teleport and expires after 8 hours. Engineers must never copy customer data to their laptops.

Security incidents are handled by the on-call security engineer, currently Carlos Diaz. The incident process follows the NIST 800-61 guide: detect, contain, eradicate, recover, and write a postmortem.

Vendors that process customer data, such as Stripe and Cloudflare, are reviewed every year by the security team.
